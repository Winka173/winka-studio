# Sample data behind the screenshots / Dữ liệu mẫu sau các ảnh chụp

The screenshots are renders of the real Flutter UI with disposable sample data (no personal data) from `apps/handy_habit/tool/store_shots_test.dart` (`sample()`), at a fixed date: **Monday 2 November 2026, 08:00**, Ho Chi Minh City time.

- Habits: Drink water (count, 8 a day), Read ten pages (amount, pages), Walk outside (minutes), Fewer sugary drinks (cut down, a limit), plus a routine "An easy morning" (3 steps) and a paused-run state for the runner.
- Every habit has about ten months of history so the yearly heatmap and month calendar look lived in.
- Mood: a few days recorded.
- Names are in each language (the Vietnamese shots use Vietnamese sample names).

Host renders have no status bar and no store connection, so the Pro page is cropped above its purchase bar. / Ảnh chụp từ trình dựng của Flutter nên không có thanh trạng thái; trang Pro đã cắt thanh mua.
