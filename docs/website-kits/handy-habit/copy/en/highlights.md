# The six blocks for the landing page (English)

1. **One tap a day.** "Today's bead is one tap away." A bead string for every habit; a hollow bead is a day, never a red mark. Image: `highlights/01-today-en.png` (dark: `01-today-dark-en.png`).
2. **Check in from the home screen.** "Tap a bead on your home screen. It fills at once." Today, One habit and Routine widgets are free. Image: `highlights/07-widgets-en.png`.
3. **Build it, or cut it down.** Limits per day or week, "none today", going over is a fresh start tomorrow. Image: `screenshots/mobile/mobile-en-03-habit-reduce.png`.
4. **Routines, step by step.** A timer ring, "Up next", one big Done; steps can check a habit in; share a routine as one page. Image: `highlights/03-runner-en.png`.
5. **See the year in your colours.** Week, month and year views, a yearly heatmap per habit, honest consistency. Image: `highlights/05-year-en.png`.
6. **Yours, on your phone.** No account, works offline, backups you control, a 30-day Trash. Image: `highlights/08-settings-en.png`.

Extras: two-tap new habit (`highlights/06-quick-add-en.png`), Pro page (`highlights/09-pro-en.png`), weekly look back (`highlights/10-week-review-en.png`), welcome with a practice day (`screenshots/mobile/mobile-en-50-welcome-3.png`).
