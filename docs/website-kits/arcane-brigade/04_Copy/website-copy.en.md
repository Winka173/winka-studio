## The pitch

**Short (1 line):** A storybook auto-battler about a little hero, a brigade of strange friends and twenty nights of things that refuse to sleep.

**Medium (store length):**
You are a little hero lost in the Waiting Room between dreams. Each night the dark sends wave after wave of strange creatures, and a boss arrives every fourth night. You move; your friends fight. Between nights you shop for new friends and relics, merge copies into stronger stars, and light the bonds between them. Reach the dawn of night twenty.

**Long:**
Arcane Brigade is a hand-painted auto-battler set in a dream that has gone on too long. You lead a single small hero through twenty nights. During a night you only steer. Your hero and every friend attack on their own, so the fight is about where you stand, what you pull after you and when you run.

Between nights the shop opens. Recruit teapots, mailboxes, moths, dice and stranger things. Every friend carries its own weapon. Buy a second copy for a half star, and a third to merge it into the next star. Friends who share a trait light up bonds, some pairs are duets that power each other up, and 23 combos let groups of two to five named friends play a joint move.

Every hero plays differently. Each has a signature skill, three ways to aim it and a transformation that changes the field for a few seconds. Bosses wait at the end of every fourth night, and each of the six dream places has tricks of its own. When a run ends, the Lamp Oil you earned lights the Lamp Tree in the Waiting Room, so the next run starts a little brighter.

## How a night works (a 4-step strip)

1. **Fight.** Survive the night. Steer your hero; your brigade attacks on its own.
2. **Shop.** Recruit friends, buy relics, refresh the stalls.
3. **Grow.** Merge copies into stars and light bonds and duets.
4. **Reach the dawn.** Twenty nights, a boss every fourth. Then the Waiting Room.

## Price and business model (state it plainly)

- **Free to play, the whole game.** All 36 heroes and all 20 nights.
- **No ads.** None, on any platform.
- **One optional purchase, Deluxe** (about US$4.99, the price set by the store): Endless mode and the two hardest difficulties, Sleepless and The Last Night. Act II will be included when it is released.
- **Nothing that makes a run stronger is ever sold.** No gems, no loot boxes, no energy.

## FAQ

**Is it really free?** Yes. The whole game, all heroes and all twenty nights, is free on phones. Deluxe adds Endless and the two hardest difficulties.

**Are there ads?** No.

**Is it pay-to-win?** No. Nothing that makes a run stronger is sold. Lamp Tree upgrades are earned by playing.

**Do I need an account or internet?** No. The game plays offline. Cloud save is optional and uses your own Google Drive.

**Which languages?** English and Vietnamese.

**Can I continue on another phone?** Yes, with cloud save turned on in Settings. Deluxe follows your Google Play account.

**How long is a run?** About half an hour for all twenty nights. You can leave at any shop and continue later.

**What do I control?** Only where your hero walks. Attacks are automatic. On phones you steer with one thumb; on PC with the keyboard or a gamepad.
