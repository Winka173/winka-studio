# Highlights (English) — the six things to put first

Use these as the landing page's feature blocks, in this order. Each one has a matching image in `highlights/` (the `-en` version) and, where noted, a clip in `clips/`.

## 1. Everything on one board
A line in your notes, a message in the class group, a scrap of paper on the desk: put each on a card. Columns say where the work is, from To do to Done. Drag it along, or use Move to… Everything can be undone.
Image: `highlights/02-board-en.png` (phone) and `highlights/02-board-desktop-en.jpg`

## 2. Colours that follow the work
Maths, Literature, Kitchen, Design: each category has its colour and stays with the card in every column. The strip shows how much of each is done. Tap one or several to see only their cards.
Image: `highlights/03-categories-en.png` · Clip: `clips/categories-en.mp4`

## 3. This week, next week, last week
Lay a board out by day, week or month with one tap. Plan next week's cards in next week. Last week stays as it was, read-only, and anything unfinished says so, with one button to bring it to this week.
Image: `highlights/04-week-en.png`, `highlights/04-week-past-en.png` · Clip: `clips/timeline-en.mp4`
*(New in the next update; see `features.md`, "Newer than the store".)*

## 4. A board ready in a minute
30 templates for school, work, home and creative projects. See the whole board before using it; answer a few questions and it fills in your subjects, dates and the fields your cards need, like a trip's cost and booking.
Image: `highlights/05-template-en.png`, `highlights/06-template-fields-en.png` · Clip: `clips/template-en.mp4`

## 5. Small steps, real reminders
Break a card into subtasks and tick them off. Set a due date, the day you'll do it and a reminder. Home and the widgets show what is due today and what was left behind.
Image: `highlights/07-card-en.png` · Clip: `clips/card-en.mp4`

## 6. Yours, on your phone
No account, no ads, no tracking. Boards stay on your device, with Trash, archive, backups to a file and automatic copies. Light and dark, English and Vietnamese.
Image: `highlights/08-dark-en.png` · Clip: `clips/dark-mode-en.mp4`

## Pro block (after the six)
"Pay once. Keep it." My Work across boards, repeating cards, automation rules, saved filters, custom fields, 16 more templates, every cover and look, designed exports, insights and history.
Image: `highlights/09-pro-my-work-en.png`, `highlights/10-pro-rules-en.png`, `highlights/11-pro-export-desktop-en.jpg` · Clip: `clips/pro-en.mp4` (the Pro page playing its own demos)
