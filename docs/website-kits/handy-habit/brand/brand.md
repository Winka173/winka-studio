# Handy Habit: brand

**Mark:** the kept/due ring of Today with today's checked bead in the middle. Cream (`#FFF5ED`) on vermilion (`#B42318`); the "still due" part of the ring is a lighter vermilion (`#D06252`). Files in `icon/` (master 1024 full-bleed, rounded 1024, 512 for Play, 192, maskable 192/512/1024) and `favicon/`. Don't recolour, outline or add effects; keep clear space of one bead radius.

**Feeling:** warm paper, calm, never alarming. A bead string is the signature; a missed day is hollow, never red.

**Colour tokens** (`colors.json`)
- Light: paper `#FAF7F2`, surface `#FFFFFF`, raised `#F1E9DF`, ink `#291C19`, muted `#66534C`, line `#E4D8CD`, anchor (oxblood) `#54261F`, vermilion `#B42318`, tint `#F9E4DC`, ochre `#805500`.
- Dark: ground `#151312`, surface `#211D1B`, raised `#302824`, ink `#FFF5ED`, muted `#CCB9AC`, line `#483B34`, anchor `#F1C5AF`, vermilion `#F07755`, tint `#42271F`, ochre `#E6BE70`.
- Habit colours: the user's own (blue, green, ochre, purple, vermilion, teal); never red for "missed".

**Type:** Nunito (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold; OFL licence in `fonts/`). Greeting 34 to 40 px bold, titles 21 to 22 px, body 15.5 px, buttons 16 px bold.

**Shape:** cards 16 px radius, white on paper, no shadows at rest; pills and chips fully round; primary button oxblood (dark mode: pale peach).

**Imagery:** real app screens only (`screenshots/`, `highlights/`); phones in a plain dark frame; no stock photos, no fake devices with status bars, no invented ratings or reviews.

**Voice:** short, kind, plain. "Small things, kept." / "Việc nhỏ, giữ đều." Never "streak lost", "failed" or "you missed".
