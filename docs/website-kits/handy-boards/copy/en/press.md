## 20 words

Handy Boards is an offline Kanban app for school, home and projects: categories, subtasks, reminders and weeks you can plan.

## 60 words

Handy Boards puts every piece of work on a card and every card on a board. Columns show where work is, colours show what it belongs to, and a board can be laid out by day, week or month. Thirty templates start a semester, a trip or a sprint in a minute. No account, no ads; everything stays on the phone.

## 150 words

Work arrives from everywhere: a line in your notes, a message in the class group, a scrap of paper on the desk. Handy Boards gathers it onto boards you can see at a glance. Each card is one piece of work; columns say how far along it is; categories, such as a subject, a project or a room, give it a colour that follows it into every column.

Any board can be planned over time with one tap. Cards added in next week belong to next week; a week gone by stays as it was, read-only, and unfinished cards say so, with one button to bring them forward. Thirty templates for study, work, home and creative projects ask a few questions and build the board, including the fields its cards need.

There is no account and no advertising, and the boards stay on the device. An optional one-time Pro purchase adds automation, repeating cards, My Work and designed exports.
