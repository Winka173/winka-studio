# Highlights (English) — the six things to put first

Use these as the landing page's feature blocks, in this order. Each one has a matching image in `highlights/` (the `-en` version) and, where noted, a clip in `clips/`.

## 1. Share it. It's saved.
Tap Share in Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X, Threads, Reddit or your browser, then Handy Bookmark. It's saved that instant, and a small sheet shows its picture and title. Tap a collection and it's filed: "Saved to Recipes". You never leave the app you were in.
Image: `highlights/01-share-sheet-en.png` · Clip: `clips/share-sheet-en.mp4`

## 2. Pictures, not addresses
Every reel, video, post and article arrives with its own title and picture, so your library looks like the things you saved, not a list of links. Screenshots and photos sit right beside them.
Image: `highlights/02-home-en.png` (phone) and `highlights/02-home-desktop-en.png`

## 3. Collections that look like yours
Recipes, Trips, To buy, Read later… Give each one an icon and a colour, nest smaller ones inside, choose a cover from its own pictures. New saves wait in Not sorted until one tap files them.
Image: `highlights/03-collection-en.png` · Clip: `clips/file-en.mp4`, `clips/collection-en.mp4`

## 4. Find it in a moment
Type a few letters, accents optional: "banh xeo" finds Bánh xèo. Search titles, sites, tags and notes.
Image: `highlights/04-search-en.png` · Clip: `clips/search-en.mp4`

## 5. Actually look at what you saved
Focus shows one unopened save at a time, full screen: swipe right when done, left for later. The Reader gives you clean text and an offline copy. Remind me brings one item back at the time you pick, and only that one.
Images: `highlights/05-focus-en.png`, `highlights/06-reader-en.png` · Clip: `clips/focus-en.mp4`

## 6. Yours, on your phone
No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Back up to a file, keep automatic backups in a folder you choose (password optional), lock the app with your fingerprint. Bring your links from your browser, Pocket or Raindrop.
Image: `highlights/07-dark-en.png` (dark mode) and `highlights/08-backup-en.png`

## Pro, in one line
"Make your library yours": 10 themes, 8 collection layouts, Focus skins, highlights with notes, smart collections, auto organize, deep search and designed PDF exports. One payment, no subscription.
Images: `highlights/09-pro-layouts-en.png`, `highlights/10-pro-highlights-en.png` · Clip: `clips/layouts-en.mp4`
