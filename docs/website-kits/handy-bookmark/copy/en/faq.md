# FAQ (English)

Answers checked against the app and `legal/privacy.md` on 2026-11-08. Suitable for a `FAQPage` JSON-LD block.

**Is Handy Bookmark free?**
Yes. Saving links and pictures, collections, search, Focus, the reader, backups and Recently deleted are free, with no ads. Handy Bookmark Pro is an optional one-time purchase for themes, layouts, highlights, smart collections, auto organize, deep search and designed exports.

**Is Pro a subscription?**
No. You pay once through Google Play and keep it. There is nothing to renew or cancel. Reinstalling, or using another device with the same Google account, restores it (Settings › Upgrade to Pro › Restore purchase).

**How much is Pro?**
The price Google Play shows before you buy, in your currency (99,000 đ in Vietnam at launch; Google Play sets the equivalent elsewhere).

**Do I need an account?**
No account and no sign-in. Open the app and start saving.

**Where is my library kept?**
In the app's private storage on your phone. The developer has no server and receives nothing from the app.

**Does the app send anything over the internet?**
Only to show a link's title and picture: it asks that page's own website for the page, the way a browser would, with no cookies, no sign-in and nothing from your library. You can switch this off in Settings. Reading copies and link checks run only when you ask. Nothing else is sent.

**How do I save from Facebook, TikTok or YouTube?**
Tap Share in that app, then Handy Bookmark (in Facebook you may need to tap More first). It's saved at once; tap a collection in the small sheet to file it, or close the sheet and it waits in Not sorted. Tip: press and hold Handy Bookmark in the share menu and pin it so it's always first.

**Why doesn't a link show a picture?**
Some pages don't give one to apps: private or age-restricted posts, pages behind a sign-in, and Reddit (title only). The link is always saved anyway, and the app tries again later.

**Can I save screenshots and photos?**
Yes. Use the + button on Home, or share a picture from your gallery to Handy Bookmark. With Pro you can copy the text in a picture, read on the device.

**I used Pocket. Can I bring my saves?**
Yes. Export from Pocket (CSV or HTML), then Settings › Data › Import and export › Import from Pocket. Read state, dates and tags come along, into a "Pocket" collection. Raindrop and browser bookmarks (HTML) import too. You see what the file holds before anything is added.

**How do I back up, or move to a new phone?**
Settings › Data › Back up makes one file with everything; keep it somewhere safe, then use Restore on the new phone. You can also turn on automatic backups to a folder you choose, optionally locked with a password.

**What if I delete something by mistake?**
Tap Undo in the message, or find it in Recently deleted for 30 days.

**Can I lock the app?**
Yes. App lock asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when the app opens.

**Does it remind me to look at what I saved?**
Only when you ask: tap Remind me on an item (or under "Saved" when you share) and pick a time. Off is the default, and the app never reminds you on its own. To look back over what you saved, Home shows what is waiting and Focus shows it one at a time.

**Can I read articles offline?**
Yes. Open an article, tap Read, then save an offline copy. Many social-media pages and pages behind a sign-in or paywall have no readable text; then the app opens the original.

**Is it in Vietnamese?**
Yes, the whole app is in Vietnamese and English. Search works with or without accents.

**Which devices does it run on?**
Android, through Google Play (currently in closed testing). An iPhone version, a Windows app and a web app are being prepared but are not available yet.

**Does it sync between devices?**
Not in the current store version. Use a backup file to move your library. (Google Drive sync between Android devices is in development and will be announced when it has been verified.)

**Is my data used for ads or sold?**
No. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer collects nothing.

**How do I contact you?**
Settings › Send feedback, or email apps.winka.studio@gmail.com.
