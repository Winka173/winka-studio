# Handy Habit: features (English)

Everything below was checked against the app's code (Handy Habit 1.0.0, build 5). Android only. "Pro" marks what needs the one-time Pro purchase; everything else is free. Screenshot names are in `screenshots/` (see README: `<form>-<lang>-<NN>-<scene>.png`).

## Today (`01-today`)
- Habits as **bead strings**: each habit shows its last six days as beads and, at the end, **today's bead**. One tap on it checks in; **Undo** is in the message that appears.
- Habits are grouped by **morning, afternoon, evening and any time**, and by collection on the habits page.
- A habit can be done-or-not, a **number of times**, **minutes** or an **amount** (pages, glasses, km). Counted habits fill their bead as a ring; minutes and amounts ask how much, with the rest of the target one tap away.
- A header shows the day: "1 of 3 kept" in a ring, and the date. A missed day is a **hollow bead, never a red mark**.
- **Mood in one tap**: five faces on Today; one tap saves it, the pencil opens energy, a note and tags.
- Routines planned for today sit under the habits with Start or Resume.
- A new habit or routine is **two taps**: New habit → type a name or tap a starter → the editor opens filled in (`05-quick-add`).

## Habits you want to cut down (`03-habit-reduce`)
- Choose **Break a habit**: fewer sugary drinks, less late-night snacking, less phone.
- A limit per day or per week, or "none at all". One tap logs one time; "None today" records an explicit zero. Going over is shown calmly and tomorrow is a fresh start; blank days are never counted as success.

## One habit (`02-habit`)
- Today first (progress, the bead string, today's bead), then streak, longest, "done x of y".
- A **month calendar in the habit's colour**; tap a day within the allowed window (up to 90 days back by default) to fill it in. A list view for narrow screens.
- Schedules (every day, some weekdays, days a week, every few days), a start and end date, a group, a priority, a note.
- **Icon and colour** from a sheet; a **reminder** with a time right in the editor.
- History of what was recorded, one row per day.

## Pauses
- **Days off** for everything or for some habits. They are not missed, keep your streaks and send no reminders. Add them in Settings › Pauses; Today says "Paused until Thursday" and offers End early.

## Routines (`07-routines`, `08-routine`, `09-runner`)
- Put steps in order with a time for each; choose weekdays and a start time.
- The **runner** is full screen: the step's name, its clock in a **ring that fills over the planned time**, "Up next", one wide Done button, Pause and Skip. Leaving pauses the run; coming back resumes it. A summary at the end.
- Steps can count for a habit: finishing the step checks the habit in.
- **Share a routine** as one page any browser can read; **import** one a friend sends (Routines › New routine › Import). Only the routine travels; never your habits, records or mood.
- 11 routine templates (morning, wind down, study, workout and more).
- Pro: steps on selected weekdays, steps that fit the time left, steps that skip when their habit is done, **A or B** paths, dependencies, follow-a-habit, different minutes by weekday, reusable step blocks and saved plans.

## Progress (`10-progress-month`, `11-progress-year`, `12-week-review`)
- **Week, month, year**; a yearly heatmap in each habit's colour; current and longest streaks; honest consistency figures ("6 of 7 days kept"); a weekly look back with one small step.
- Export a range as **CSV, Markdown or PDF**.
- Pro: compare months, quarters or groups; difficulty and missed-weekday patterns; small goal suggestions you accept or dismiss; mood beside habits; focused heatmaps; a designed review on paper.

## Mood
- Optional. Five faces, then energy (1 to 5), a note and tags if you want. History in Progress; edit or delete; deleted entries can be restored. It never appears in notifications or widgets.

## Home-screen widgets (`23-widgets`) — Android
- **Today** (up to five habits with their bead strings), **One habit**, **Routine** are free. **This week** and **16 weeks** are Pro; without Pro they show dimmed with a line saying so.
- **Tap a bead on the home screen to check in**: it fills at once and the day is saved a moment later. Counts add one per tap; minutes, amounts, full or skipped days open the app.
- New day starts by itself after midnight. Names can be hidden; mood and notes are never included. Add from Settings › Home-screen widgets.

## Reminders
- Per habit or routine, on the days it is planned; quiet hours; the lock screen does not show habit names by default; a "Why didn't my reminder appear?" page.

## Templates (`06-templates`)
- **44 habit templates** and **11 routine templates** in 13 collections (sleep, move, focus, reading, water, finance, less phone and more). Open one: the editor opens filled in; change anything before Save.

## Your data
- On your device, no account. **Backups**: ZIP, JSON, password-locked; restore with a preview and a recovery copy; CSV import.
- **Back up automatically**: a copy a day when something changed, the last seven kept on the device.
- **Trash** keeps deleted habits and routines 30 days.
- **Google Drive sync** (optional, Android): to your own Drive's hidden app folder; off until you switch it on.

## Look and language
- **English and Vietnamese**; light, dark or follow the phone; compact or comfortable; follows the phone's reduce-motion setting.
- Pro: personalization studio (palettes, Today layouts, extra icons, runner looks), rules ("after my morning routine, add a glass of water", with Undo) and batch changes with a preview.

## Help
- A short welcome with a **practice day that changes nothing** (`50-welcome-1`…), and **How to use** with eleven topics, each a sheet of numbered steps with pictures (`24-how-to`, `25-how-to-topic`).
