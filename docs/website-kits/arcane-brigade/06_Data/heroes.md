# Heroes (24)

Every hero has a signature skill (its name in capitals), three ways to aim it, and a transformation. Art: `07_Art/heroes/<name>.png`. Skill picture: `02_Screenshots/pc/skill-NN-<Id>.jpg`.

Twelve more heroes are finished but held back for content updates and Acts II and III; they are left out on purpose, so the website must not mention or show them.

## Lantern Knight

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/lantern-knight.png`
- In one line: A keyblade-bearing pioneer. Travel to charge a lantern pulse; build a bustling brigade.
- Skill: **THE GUIDING LAMP** (gauge: Company light)
- How it works: Nearby friends' basic attacks kindle the lamp (one spark per 0.25s). At 8 sparks, up to four friends join a finite-budget lantern advance under its light. Stay within 4 units to gather sparks. Alone, your own attacks light it. Approach: a focused line, a wide fan, or a protective circle that also snuffs one hostile shot.
- Three ways to aim it: Advance, Fan out, Hold ground

## Postmaster

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/postmaster.png`
- In one line: A wandering parcel launcher. Collected gold charges automatic express volleys.
- Skill: **SIGNED DELIVERY** (gauge: Addresses)
- How it works: Basic hits stamp up to three different enemies for 6s. Every third personal attack delivers parcels to the stamped addresses; homing parcels share 90% weapon damage. Choose express parcels, fragile ember packages, or a linking relay. Without a stamp, the next target receives the delivery.
- Three ways to aim it: Express, Fragile, Relay

## Drowsy Seer

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/drowsy-seer.png`
- In one line: A sleepy tuning-bell oracle. Stand still to chill nearby threats and erase a few hostile bullets.
- Skill: **THE DREAM THAT WAITS** (gauge: Drowsiness)
- How it works: Attacking and walking fill 4 dream sparks. When they are full, pause for half a second (or keep going for 3s) and a dream of you stays behind, waiting where you were; it attacks from there for 4s while you move on. Rest healing remains. Choose a focused dream, a spreading dream, or a chilling dream. One dream at a time; no extra body in the roster.
- Three ways to aim it: Lucid, Wandering, Winter

## The Root Oracle

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/the-root-oracle.png`
- In one line: Stationary in combat. Whole-arena pickup and a slowing ward. Walks in shops.
- Skill: **THE LIVING GARDEN** (gauge: Root cycle)
- How it works: Rooted in battle, with three planted roots around you. Every 3s an occupied root attacks or shelters you. Roots can catch a hostile shot in Shelter, rake a line in Thorns, or trade some attack power for recovery in Harvest. You still walk normally in shops.
- Three ways to aim it: Shelter, Thorns, Harvest

## The Lone Jelly

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/the-lone-jelly.png`
- In one line: Travels without roster companions; absorbs purchased companions' traits. Strong attacks and natural recovery support relic-focused builds.
- Skill: **THE MEMORY BODY** (gauge: Organ rhythm)
- How it works: Equipped memories become three rotating organs. Every third attack expresses the next organ: a bite, a frost gland, or a stitched limb based on the memory's traits. Select memories in the shop; all previously absorbed passive traits remain. With no memory, the mouth supplies a short bite.
- Three ways to aim it: Bite, Glands, Threadwork

## Lady Loaded Dice

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/lady-loaded-dice.png`
- In one line: Collect 6 gold to burst nearby enemies. High luck and critical chance; low health.
- Skill: **THE LOADED HAND** (gauge: Collected gold)
- How it works: Collect 6 gold to play the visible next die face, with a once every 4s. Faces cast darts, a cross, frost, a coin, healing, or a jackpot. The next face follows a visible six-face sequence. In shops, pay 6 gold to reroll the starting face once per wave. Approach changes the geometry of damage faces.
- Three ways to aim it: Straight bet, Split bet, Around the table

## The Tide Singer

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/the-tide-singer.png`
- In one line: Collect 8 gold to heal 3 health and push nearby enemies back.
- Skill: **THE THREE-INGREDIENT TIDE** (gauge: Ingredients)
- How it works: Every 3 coin pickups mix a tide, at most once per 4s. Every two drinks bought add an extra note, up to three. Choose an aimed current, a restorative shore, or a breaking wave. The restorative shore heals 3 HP; ordinary enemies may be pushed, bosses stay anchored.
- Three ways to aim it: Current, Shore, Breaker

## The Comet Courier

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/the-comet-courier.png`
- In one line: Travel 10 units to release a slowing pulse. Moving charges it; shops do not.
- Skill: **THE COMET'S WAKE** (gauge: Distance)
- How it works: Travel 10 world units to ignite the last three points of your route as a delayed wake. Your path determines its shape. Choose a narrow trail, parallel crescents, or a closed loop. Damage is shared across the whole wake; shops and pauses cannot charge it.

## The Mirror Keeper

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/the-mirror-keeper.png`
- In one line: Stand still for 2 seconds to reflect up to 3 nearby bullets. Moving resets charge.
- Skill: **THE BORROWED VOLLEY** (gauge: Stored reflections)
- How it works: Nothing is taken, only borrowed: the mirror catches a hostile shot that comes within 1 unit of you (2 units after you stand still for half a second), one every 2s, up to three. Your next basic attack returns them, or they return after 2s. Each returned shot carries 60% W. Choose an aimed fan, reverse trajectories, or a ring.
- Three ways to aim it: Aimed glass, Return to sender, Prism ring

## The Treasure Clerk

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/the-treasure-clerk.png`
- In one line: Every 10 collected gold grants 1 extra gold and a small healing pulse.
- Skill: **THE BOUNTY LEDGER** (gauge: Bounty stamps)
- How it works: A visible bounty marks one enemy. Basic hits put stamps on it; three stamps settle the claim even against a boss. A kill also settles it. Claims pay 1 gold (maximum 8 per wave) and send a bounded archive strike. Choose the closest threat, a ranged threat, or the richest-health target.
- Three ways to aim it: Local accounts, Collectors, Large accounts

## Copper Cauldron

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/copper-cauldron.png`
- In one line: Start with +8 contact damage. Each Morning Bells activation gives +0.15 armor. Taking damage adds 0.15 regeneration for this run. Enemies move 10% faster.
- Skill: **PRESSURE COOKER** (gauge: Steam)
- How it works: Nearby enemies build steam; taking an ordinary hit adds two steam. At 6 steam, vent a directional jet. Lid catches a nearby bullet, Boil makes a wide cone, and Whistle draws a long steam line. Hunger and other health costs do not fuel the kettle.
- Three ways to aim it: Lid, Boil, Whistle

## China Marionette

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/china-marionette.png`
- In one line: Healing is 2.5 times as effective, but armor starts 0.25 lower. Each Wild Stitch activation adds 0.4 regeneration. Taking damage adds 1 contact damage.
- Skill: **THE REPAIRED DOLL** (gauge: Porcelain shards)
- How it works: Every crack becomes a piece: each ordinary injury, and every 6 HP actually recovered, grows a porcelain shard (up to three). An attack spends one shard on a delayed counterpiece. Choose a needle, a broken mirror, or a sheltering stitch. Sheltering stitches heal 1 HP but cannot grow another shard from their own healing.
- Three ways to aim it: Needlework, Broken mirror, Sheltering stitch

## Soap Skater

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/soap-skater.png`
- In one line: Ordinary attacks deal 35% less damage. Push ordinary enemies harder, within the push cap. Each Clockwork Union activation adds 4 wave income. Treasure +20%.
- Skill: **BUBBLE CALLIGRAPHY** (gauge: Turns)
- How it works: Changing travel direction by at least 55 degrees while moving leaves a bubble, at most once per 1.2s. Bubbles pop after a short warning, slowing or gently pushing ordinary enemies. Three recent turns form a larger pattern. Choose a ribbon, a fountain, or a round bubble.
- Three ways to aim it: Ribbon, Fountain, Bubble

## Velvet Regent

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/velvet-regent.png`
- In one line: You do not attack. Your personal damage, attack speed, critical chance and projectile bonuses also help allies. Shops may sell evolved companions. More enemies appear.
- Skill: **THE THREE COMMANDS** (gauge: Orders)
- How it works: You never attack yourself. Six allied basic attacks prepare a command, at most once per 4s. Spear selects the closest enemy, Volley favors ranged enemies, and Court favors the toughest enemy. Up to three friends perform their own weapon-shaped responses, sharing one budget. Your choice also changes friend target priorities.
- Three ways to aim it: Spear, Volley, Court

## Button Cavalier

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/button-cavalier.png`
- In one line: Start with 35% dodge and 35% less HP. Dodge cap: 75%. Dodges add treasure detection for this run. Toy Knights deal 30% more damage.
- Skill: **THE COURTEOUS RIPOSTE** (gauge: Poise)
- How it works: Answer every discourtesy with a bow: a near miss gives 1 poise, a dodge 2, and an ordinary blow that lands on you 1. Each bullet can grant poise once; near misses have a 0.8s shared cooldown. At 3 poise, your next attack makes a precise riposte. Choose a thrust, a sweeping guard, or a crossed salute.
- Three ways to aim it: Thrust, Guard sweep, Salute

## Night Maw

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/night-maw.png`

- Skill: **THE HUNGRY MOUTH** (gauge: Meals)
- How it works: Kills feed the mouth. Three meals prepare a bite against nearby enemies, with a once every 2.5s. Choose a focused bite, a sweeping jaw, or a rear maw. Hunger and kill healing remain; bosses cannot be swallowed or instantly executed.

## Thorn Treasurer

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/thorn-treasurer.png`
- In one line: Dream shops follow the shared 3-4-wave schedule. At wave start, maximum HP follows saved gold (20–400). Buying a spiked relic recruits a Bristle Ball. Team damage -20%.
- Skill: **THE COLLATERAL CROWN** (gauge: Collateral thorns)
- How it works: Saved gold gives up to four collateral thorns at wave start (one per 30 gold). Each ordinary injury spends one thorn to answer with a directed thorn pattern; no gold is secretly spent. Basic attacks regrow one thorn every four attacks, and a full crown spends one by itself on an enemy that comes within 2.5 units. Choose lances, a crown, or a crossed receipt. Your gold-based HP and dream-shop rule remain.
- Three ways to aim it: Lances, Crown, Crossed receipt

## Citrus Marshal

## Ivory Sovereign

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/ivory-sovereign.png`

- Skill: **THE LAST DECREE** (gauge: Royal seals)

Decrees take the shape of a verdict line, a court seal, or a crossed sentence. Retaliation is delayed and has a 1s shared cooldown; health costs do not write decrees.
- Three ways to aim it: Verdict, Court seal, Sentence

## Petal Citadel

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/petal-citadel.png`
- In one line: Start each wave with a balanced blessing: +2 flat melee and ranged damage, +10 HP, +5% speed and +0.15 armor.
- Skill: **THE FOUR-PETAL CITADEL** (gauge: Petal growth)

Its geometry and recovery depend on which petals have grown; a complete garden opens an extra outer bloom. Choose a directed bloom, a thorn lattice, or shelter petals.

## Archive Curator

## Tin Virtuoso

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/tin-virtuoso.png`
- In one line: Your shots cannot split. Allies fire only in response to your critical attacks, with their own weapon damage and a short cooldown. Start with +10% critical chance and 2 projectiles.
- Skill: **THE BRIGADE SCORE** (gauge: Beats)

## Lantern Weaver

- Art: `07_Art/heroes/lantern-weaver.png`
- In one line: Every fourth attack stitches three nearby enemies with a delayed lantern thread. The thread shares one damage budget.
- Skill: **THE LIVING NETWORK** (gauge: Knots)
- How it works: Basic hits pin up to three living targets. Every fourth attack closes the network, sharing damage over its links. Choose a closed triangle, a stitch toward your position, or a broad seam. Knots last 6s and broken targets are removed; no infinite linked-hit chain.
