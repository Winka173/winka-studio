# FAQ (English)

Answers checked against the app and `legal/privacy.md` on 2026-11-09. Suitable for a `FAQPage` JSON-LD block.

**Is Handy Boards free?**
Yes. Boards, cards, categories, subtasks, drag and drop, due dates, reminders, the timeline, search, backups and plain export are free, with no ads and no limit on boards or cards. Handy Boards Pro is an optional one-time purchase for extra templates, covers and looks, repeating cards, automation, My Work, designed exports and insights.

**Is Pro a subscription?**
No. You pay once through Google Play and keep it. There is nothing to renew or cancel. Reinstalling, or using another device with the same Google account, restores it (Settings › Upgrade to Pro › Restore).

**How much is Pro?**
The price Google Play shows before you buy, in your currency (99,000 đ in Vietnam and US$3.89 in the United States at launch; Google Play sets the equivalent elsewhere).

**What happens to my boards if Pro goes away?**
Nothing you made is deleted. Pro looks fall back to the defaults and come back with Pro; values in your own fields stay readable; repeating cards keep their dates.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-in. Open the app and make a board.

**Where are my boards kept?**
In the app's private storage on your device. The developer has no server and receives nothing. Backups are files you make and keep yourself.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes, completely. Nothing in the app needs the internet except Google Play's own purchase screen, and Google Drive sync if you switch it on.

**Can I use it on two phones?**
Yes, with the optional Google Drive sync (Settings › Data › Sync, Android): your boards go to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive and reach your other Android devices on the same account. Without sync, move boards with a backup file.

**Is it for school or for work?**
Both, and for home. The welcome asks what you mostly organise (study, work, or neither) only to suggest examples and templates; every template is open to everyone.

**What is a category?**
A colour for what a card is about: a subject, a project, a room, a person. It follows the card into every column. Tap one or several categories in the strip to see only their cards.

**How do I plan by week?**
Tap **Time** on the board and choose By day, By week or By month. Use ‹ and › to move between periods. Cards you add in next week are planned for next week. Past periods are read-only; unfinished cards there are marked and can be brought to this week with one tap.

**Can I change a weekly board to monthly, or turn it off?**
You can move to a longer period (day → week → month), not back to a shorter one, so no card lands in the wrong place. Turning the timeline off changes no card; you can archive cards already done at the same time. Turned on again, every card is back in its period.

**What is the difference between a due date and the planned day?**
The due date is the deadline. The planned day is when you mean to do it. Moving work to today, or bringing it to this week, changes only the planned day, never the due date.

**Can a card repeat, like a meeting every Monday?**
Yes, with Pro: daily, weekly (on the weekdays you choose), monthly, every few days, with an end if you want one. When it is done, the next one appears; on a timeline board it lands in the next period.

**Will it remind me?**
Yes. Set a reminder on any card. In Settings › Notifications you can also switch on today's work, due soon, left behind and quiet hours. "Hide card names" keeps titles off the lock screen.

**Are there home-screen widgets?**
Yes: Today, Board progress and Left behind & coming up are free; My Work and Week come with Pro. Long-press your home screen › Widgets › Handy Boards.

**What are fields?**
Extra details on every card of a board: a cost, a link, booked yes/no, an owner. Many templates bring suitable fields that are free to fill; making your own fields is Pro.

**Can I bring my Trello boards?**
Yes. Export a board from Trello as JSON and import it in Settings › Data; you see what will be added before anything changes. CSV files (with your own column mapping) and plain task lists work too.

**Can I export a board?**
Yes: a picture or PDF of the board (plain style free; poster, printable and report with Pro), CSV, Markdown, an ICS calendar, or a full backup.

**What if I delete something by mistake?**
Undo appears after every change. Deleted cards wait in Trash until you empty it, finished work can be archived instead of deleted, and automatic backups keep the last seven copies on the device.

**Which platforms?**
Android today, in early access on Google Play. iPhone, Windows and web versions are not released.

**Which languages?**
English and Vietnamese, chosen in Settings (or following the phone).
