# Handy Habit: press kit (English)

**Fact sheet**
- Name: Handy Habit · Maker: Winka Studio · Contact: apps.winka.studio@gmail.com
- Platform: Android (package `com.apps.winka.handyhabit`), Google Play closed testing. No iOS, Windows or web release.
- Languages: English, Vietnamese · Themes: light, dark
- Price: free; optional one-time Pro (no subscription). No account, no ads in this version, no analytics.
- Version: 1.0.0 (build 5) · Category: Productivity

**20 words:** Handy Habit turns good intentions into bead strings that fill day by day. Check in with one tap, even from your home screen.

**60 words:** Handy Habit is an Android habit tracker built around one idea: a string of beads, one a day. Tap today's bead to check in, from the app or straight from a home-screen widget. Count glasses, pages or minutes, cut a habit down, run routines step by step with a timer ring. No account, works offline, your data stays on your phone.

**150 words:** Good intentions fade: a sticky note to drink water, a reading streak dropped, a calendar left empty. Handy Habit turns them into bead strings that fill day by day. Today's bead is one tap away, in the app or on a home-screen widget that fills the moment you tap it. A missed day is a hollow bead, never a red mark; your longest streak and honest consistency still tell the story.

Build a habit or cut one down with a daily or weekly limit. Run routines full screen, with a timer ring, "Up next" and a summary, and share a routine with a friend as one page. See the week, month and year, with a yearly heatmap in each habit's colour.

There is no account and no server of ours: habits stay on the phone, with backups you control, a 30-day Trash and optional sync to your own Google Drive. English and Vietnamese, light and dark. Pro is one payment for extras; tracking and your data are always free.

**Boilerplate:** Winka Studio makes small, calm, local-first apps and games. Handy Habit, Handy Boards and Handy Bookmark are part of the Handy family.
