# The sample boards in the screenshots

Every screenshot and clip shows the same five boards, built by `apps/handy_boards/tool/website_shots_test.dart` (`kitSample`) from the app's own templates with their example cards, in Vietnamese or English. The day is fixed: **Wednesday 11 November 2026, 9:00**, so the week bar reads 9/11 – 15/11.

| Board | Template | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Học kỳ 1 / Semester 1 | Semester Planner | Laid out **by week**. Four subjects (Web Development, Databases, English, Advanced Maths), four extra cards with due dates, one card left over from last week (so the past week shows "Not finished"), and a mock test planned for next week. Key date: semester ends 15 Jan 2027. |
| Đà Nẵng 4 ngày / Da Nang, 4 days | Trip Preparation | The template's fields: Cost filled in on five cards (đ amounts), Booked ticked on three. |
| Việc nhà tháng 11 / Home in November | Personal Tasks | Two repeating cards: rent (monthly) and the bins (weekly). |
| Ra mắt website / Website launch | Product Launch | Launch on 1 Dec 2026. |
| Sprint 24 | Development Sprint | Points and Owner fields from the template. |

Nothing in the sample is a real person, customer or review. Card titles are ordinary student and household tasks written for the kit.

Pro screens (marked `pro: true` in `captions.json`) were rendered with Pro switched on in the test; free screens with it off. The automation rule, saved filter ("Maths, due soon") and card template ("Lab report") were added for those screens only.
